Modern World

… is an adventure created by Gwannon for John Harper's Lady Blackbird RPG.

Modern World is a resort and amusement park where the richest people of Ilysium go to amuse themselves. The park features electric automatons, known as mannequins, that evoke the future described in the cheap, pulp-style science fiction novels, a world where steam and coal have given way to electricity and solar sails.

The Empire has recognized the potential for military use of the inventions developed by August Thornberry, owner of Modern World, and his group of goblin engineers, but they have refused to sell their technology to the empire.

The Empire does not take rejection well. If it isn’t handed over willingly, it will have to send a destroyer to take it by force.

What no one knows is that Thornberry is no genius inventor. He is merely a charlatan with a silver tongue, hired by the goblins Yalp, Snot and Dez,the true electrical geniuses, to serve as the public face of the park and to sign for bank loans. No bank in Ilysium would ever provide funding for the mad ideas of three goblins.

Before the Devastator enters the planetoid’s atmosphere and its ground assault forces conquest Modern World, they must destroy the park’s facilities, ensuring the Empire cannot get its hands on the technological discoveries that give this place its magic.

But it doesn’t end there; they will have to escape from the Devastator, for if any of the goblins are taken prisoner, they will be forced to reveal their scientific secrets by any means necessary, even through torture.

The Amusement Park

Modern World is one of a kind. It is, as the press once titled it, «The most incredible place in the Empire». Every year, thousands of rich persons visit it and upon returning home, they can speak only in glowing terms to those they know.

The planetoid on which the park has been built has an entire system of solar sails that power a massive space engine, making the planetoid, in effect, a fully functional, but extremely slow, starship.

It has a spacious spaceport where the rich and powerful people of Ilysium can land their ostentatious ships—after paying exorbitant landing fees.

From there, they can access the resort, where they are accommodated in their luxurious suites. After freshening up in their rooms or enjoying a nice meal, they can enter the park and experience its wonders, such as the Thunderbird Flight in West World or the Kevlar Labyrinth in East World.

At the resort, there are only humans, chefs, waiters, attendants, cleaning staff, belboy and so on. In contrast, the park contains only autonomous, motorized mannequins tasked with enlivening the various attractions and shows, as well as selling food or souvenirs.

There are only a few humans and goblins dedicated to the maintenance of mannequins and attractions, or to more delicate tasks such as the massages and beauty treatments offered at the thermal baths.

The park is divided into 5 zones: one central zone, with the other 4 arranged around it to the north, south, east and west. Each zone of the park is themed after a series of cheap, 5-credit books, the kind the goblins used to devour in their childhood and freetime.

Modern World

This zone, which gives the park its name, is located in the center. It represents our world, but in an electric future.

There are mannequins that polish your shoes, others that sell newspapers and leaflets, others that pull rickshaws and take you wherever you want to go, others that trim hedges and so on. There are also many that walk along the streets, entering and leaving buildings as if they were office workers, accountants, or secretaries arriving at or leaving their workplaces.

Every so often, there is a parade of mannequins, complete with a mannequin marching band and decorated platforms, celebrating the benefits of electricity and how it is produced.

North World

It is a world of gods and heroes from ancient myths. There are marble temples, mosaics, hidden crypts and sumptuous thermal baths where visitors relax and enjoy therapeutic treatments, such as massages and libations.

Visitors are treated like heroes, with praises and delicious plates.

Military parades, electric-chariot races and electrified gladiator combat are very commons.

South World

This zone is a vast zoo featuring electric animatronic figures of many species of wild animals.

There are areas of jungle, savanna, steppe and even tundra, a multitude of ecosystems that have been extinct for years.

In these regions, one finds mammoths, bison, large felines, birds of every color and massive reptiles with even larger teeth and claws.

East World

In the east are East World, a pastiche of myths and tales drawn from the farthest reaches of known space, featuring alien princes and princesses, strange, gargantuan beasts of burden or war and exotic palaces with secret passageways.

There are duels fought with electro-swords while impossible acrobatics are developed, bursts of flame and bolts of energy, as well as powerful seers who can read your fortune.

And as great finale there are dances accompanied by the screeching of countless metallic joints and gears.

West World

In the western section lies West World, a sort of futuristic frontier featuring

cowboys mounted on android horses, cows equipped with solar panels from which you can «milk» electricity and electrical ray guns.

Shootings at dawn, bank robberies, train heists and saloon brawls are the typical attractions in this area, not to mention the "Flight of the Thunderbird", the most mind-blowing attraction ever created by man (or goblins).

Moving between zones

Modern World is located in the center of the park, while the other worlds are situated to the north, south, east, and west, as their names suggest. This distribution was chosen to help visitors orient themselves easily. Consequently, moving from one zone to another always requires passing through Modern World.

The easiest way to get around is on the surface, but there is also an underground network of tunnels and corridors beneath the park, used for maintenance. Navigating in the underground is complex, yet it offers an ideal way to move about safely and discreetly, out of sight.

High tech

The electronic technology used in the park by Yalp, Snot, and Dez is far superior to that of the other scientific researchers in the Empire.

For one thing, their electrical systems and solar sails are twice as powerful as the steam and coal power used by the rest of the world. Their inventions can operate for days without needing to be recharged, thanks to solar sails that convert light into electricity.

Their electric mannequins feature incredible engineering that perfectly replicates the muscles and bones of humans and animals. There are even movements that these electric mannequins perform better and faster than humans.

The third and most important technological development is their electronic brains, which surpass the mechanical thinking machines used by the Empire. These electronic brains are significantly smaller in size and can be programmed in a much wider variety of ways, using more complex programs, for instance, by programming the generation of voices through loudspeaker systems.

It is interesting that Yalp, Snot, and Dez, rather than selling their inventions to the highest bidder, used them to create a place for fun and entertainment. This is what most surprised the Empire’s high-ranking officials when they first laid eyes on these new electronic technologies.

The goblins consider all this technology to be their own, and the idea of ​​it falling into the hands of others to be used for military purposes is inconceivable to them.

It is clear that they would destroy it rather than hand it over to the Empire. They might even sacrifice themselves to prevent it from falling into the wrong hands.

The plan

Once the park has been evacuated, your players should look for a way to prevent the Imperial Fleet from getting their technology. If it falls into the wrong hands, like those of the Empire, it could be a disaster.

Your players created this technology to help people and bring them happiness and, of course, to make some money. So, before fleeing the planetoid, they must destroy it.

August will always be the main target of the Devastator’s assault troops. As everyone knows, he is the scientific genius behind all the technology in the park. If they capture him, they will be able to extract all the secrets they desire. According to their reports, the goblins are merely laboratory assistants.

The only available plan to destroy their technology is to go to each zone and reach its control center. From each of these, you can deactivate all the mannequins and attractions in that zone, and then overload them, frying their circuits and destroying them.

The final step would be to destroy each control center to ensure that the Empire can't recover their technology.

The simplest method would be to use explosives, but if they don’t have any explosive on hand, they can get creative.

  • In North World, there is volcanic activity, hence its famous hot springs.
  • South World has a gas pipeline nearby that carries gas extracted from the planetoid.
  • West World is where the park's fireworks are launched from, so there's a possibility of using it.
  • The control center of East World is located beneath the central lake. It could be flooded.
  • Modern World doesn't appear to have any weaknesses.

In Modern World, in addition to the control zone, where they will begin, they will also have to destroy the laboratory. This should be particularly difficult for the three Goblins, as it means destroying their life's work.

This section must be the last one to be destroyed, as it needs to remain functional for them to travel between different parts of the park and reach the spaceport via the shuttle train. Furthermore, they will have to destroy it remotely; if they destroy it before reaching the spaceport, the shuttle train will come to a halt in the middle of the track.

The destruction mission has a time limit. Create a clock with 9 sections; fill in one section each time a sabotage plan, whether successful or failed, is carried out. Once all sections are filled, the Devastator will land, and the assault troops will take control of whatever remains of the park.

Split the group might be an interesting option, but one of the goblins will have to work with August, who is quite useless with situations whete science is important.

The final step is to reach the spaceport and escape, because, if they are too late, all their efforts will have been for nothing. The Empire will set its best interrogators, or rather, torturers, to work, trying to extract your players' technological secrets.

The escape requires one segment of the clock, so time is very limited. To begin with, they need 5 segments of the clock to destroy the entire park, plus the one for the escape. That leaves them with only 3 possible failures.

As you will see, destroying all the zones and escaping before the Devastator’s assault troops take over the park is practically impossible. Someone will have to sacrifice himself and stay behind—or the others will have to abandon them for the good of the group. It’s not immediately apparent, but it will gradually become very real as the sections of the clock are filled in.

 

What is happen?

As your players move through the park, carrying out their plan of destruction, all sorts of things can occur. We provide an event table for each zone of the park, plus one final table for the spaceport.

2 Stragglers: The final stragglers from the evacuation wait in a throng for the last transport that will take them to the spaceport. People are very nervous and frightened.
3 Robo-psychosis: Following the sudden disappearance of the visitors, the electric mannequins have begun interacting with one another, often falling into a loop, such as repeatedly greeting each other or continually opening a shop door for one another.

5 Abandoned suitcases: Visitors have left their heavy suitcases behind as they fled. If you take the time to search them, you might find anything—and some items could prove useful.
6 Assault forces: An assault ship from the Devastator has moved ahead and deployed a brigade of stormtroopers to secure the area.

4 Reconnaissance flight: A spy shuttle from the Devastator flies over the area to report back to high command. If you are detected, your sabotage plan could fall apart.
5 Silence: By pure chance, at this moment there is no one nearby, neither people nor mannequins. You are alone and in complete silence.
6 The Laurels of Victory: In the absence of visitors, the electric mannequins begin to gather and stage a parade in honor of your players for their achievements in battle. It is difficult to move through the crowd, which cheers them on and showers them with petals and garlands.

2 Tumbleweeds: Lots of desert tumbleweeds pass away.

If any of the Devastator’s assault forces are nearby, you could set a trap for them using the gunslinger mannequins or even unleash them by programming the mannequins to hunt down and eliminate the «outlaws» arriving from the Devastator’s side. 4 Snake-Oil Tonic: A mannequin wearing a black frock coat and a top hat approaches you, offering a small glass bottle that he claims contains «snake-oil tonic». He keeps rattling on about its miraculous virtues. The strange thing is that no one recalls ever designing or programming these features and no one knows what is actually inside the bottle.
5 Electric horses: At a watering trough, there are 4 electric horses with their charge at maximum capacity. Visitors use them to get around the area, and they are also perfect for pulling heavy loads.
6 Triple X: The saloon has just received a shipment of alcoholic beverages, including several large barrels containing high-proof spirits. These can be set on fire or used to craft effective Molotov cocktails.

2 Engagement ring: You stumble upon a small red velvet box and fall face-first. If you open it, you’ll find an engagement ring that someone lost during the evacuation. Engraved on the inside are the names “Mika & Lua.” It seems someone was planning to propose to their partner during the fireworks.
3 Aliens: Electric mannequins wearing masks of extraterrestrial beings appear in the streets. They speak in an unintelligible language and constantly move about, touching everything in sight. It is a spectacle designed to entertain visitors and it has just been activated.
4 Closed attraction: A maintenance crew had closed off an attraction, and when the order was given to evacuate the park, they left all their equipment behind. There might be some interesting items there, or perhaps your players could recover something important that they had previously lost.
5 Rickshaws: There’s a parking of rickshaws pulled by electric mannequins. You just have to hop on and tell them where you want to go, and the mannequin will take off at full speed.
6 Helium: An electric mannequin carrying a large bunch of helium balloons walks nearby. Usually, it gives them away for free to passersby, who then have to immediately inflate new ones, but now, with no one wanting any, it’s starting to have trouble staying anchored to the ground.

1 Four wheeled: In the middle of the forest, there is an off-road vehicle, likely left behind by a maintenance team, that can be used to move around the area more quickly.

3 The Legend of the Ape-Man: For weeks, there have been rumors that an electric mannequin, lost from another zone, is wandering through the jungles of South World. It wears only a loincloth fashioned from the remnants of its clothing and appears to move through the treetops.
4 I want cookies!: An electric cockatoo lands on a character’s shoulder. Even if you shoo it away, it returns after a while. It constantly asks for cookies and repeats your own phrases at the most inopportune moments.
5 Flying in circles: Electric, vulture-shaped mannequins begin circling above your characters. It’s unclear what this might mean, but it doesn’t look good.

3 Seized engines: A small service craft is unable to take off. If it does not receive assistance and repairs to its engines, its 3 crew members could be injured or even killed during the landing of the Devastator’s troops.
4 Catering: You find an abandoned luxury catering cart, intended for passengers to enjoy its delicate dishes on the return flight.
5 Explosions: A loud explosion is heard in the distance. You cannot tell whether it is at the spaceport itself or in another part of the park.
6 No one is left behind: Some of the staff, the most loyal and devoted ones, refuse to leave until your players leave with them.

 

Characters

Although Modern World is owned by August Thornberry, its board of directors consists of him and the three goblin engineers who created all the park’s marvels: Yalp, Snot, and Dez.

When the Devastator appeared on the radar, the board ordered the evacuation of all park staff and visitors for their own safety; as a result, they are now the only four individuals remaining within the entire facility.

Power dynamics

Throughout the time the park has been open, power dynamics have remained very stable. Everyone knew their place, as well as the limits of their own power and influence.

All of that went out the window when the Devastator set a course for Modern World, its cannons primed and ready to fire.

August has always been the company’s figurehead, he had no real say in anything, but that has just changed. He is now a full participant in this risky task. He has the capacity to decide how he wants to act, and his opinion must be taken into account by the rest of the group. Not to mention that his skills are now of great value.

Yalp you are the oldest and most experienced of the goblins, It was your ideas that gave rise to the technology that brings the park to life. For a long time, you have been the leader of the group, but you are getting older, and your ideas are no longer as good as they once were. The younger ones are coming on strong, but they still have much to learn—and you are going to be the one to teach them.

Snott You’re not a genius, but you’re the one who takes care of fixing the little problems that no one else bothers with. Without you, nothing would keep running for two days in a row, though the others don’t seem to realize that. You might not fully grasp how the electronic brain inside a mannequin works—the thing that enables it to perform all those amazing feats, but you’ve managed to keep them running for days without needing a recharge, and that’s important, too. Honestly, a little recognition would be quite nice.

Dez is the youngest of them all, and for that reason his ideas have always been undervalued, but his potential is immense, like a supernova on the verge of exploding. Perhaps now his ideas will be heard.

Although the characters are all male, this is merely a suggestion. You are free to adapt them however you like; in fact, ask your players to define their characters' pronouns when they choose their characters.

  • Secret of Genius: You are a genius. Once per session, you may reroll a failed check involving engineering, mechanics, electricity, …

  • CONDITIONS

    • 🞏 INJURED
    • 🞏 TIRED
    • 🞏 ANGRY
    • 🞏 LOST
    • 🞏 HUNTED
    • 🞏 TRAPPED
    • 🞏 SUFFOCATED
    • 🞏 ELECTROCUTED
    • 🞏 DEAD (PRESUMED)

ITEMS: Bata de laboratorio llena de manchas de grasa, lápiz detrás de la oreja, bolsillos llenos de papeles con fórmulas e ideas, matraces y viales y algún tipo de herramienta con filo que puede usarse como arma cuerpo a cuerpo.

APPEARANCE: Con sus una gafas con cristales gruesos y su bata de laboratorio parece el típico científico despistado que no levanta los ojos de sus fórmulas y sus experimentos.
