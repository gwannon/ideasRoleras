## What is happen?

As your players move through the park, carrying out their plan of destruction, all sorts of things can occur. We provide an event table for each zone of the park, plus one final table for the spaceport.

\conc

|1d6|Modern World|
|---|---|
|1|**Clean Your Boots, Sir?:** An electric mannequin insists on cleaning your shoies and won’t take no for an answer. It will follow the group until they let it clean their shoes.|
|2|**Stragglers:** The final stragglers from the evacuation wait in a throng for the last transport that will take them to the spaceport. People are very nervous and frightened.|
|3|**Robo-psychosis:** Following the sudden disappearance of the visitors, the electric mannequins have begun interacting with one another, often falling into a loop, such as repeatedly greeting each other or continually opening a shop door for one another.|
|4|**The Oracle:** There’s a ice pops stand where the sticks feature cryptic predictions. It wouldn’t hurt to stop for a few minutes, enjoy some ice pops in your favorite flavors, and discover what your fortune holds.|
|5|**Abandoned suitcases:** Visitors have left their heavy suitcases behind as they fled. If you take the time to search them, you might find anything—and some items could prove useful.|
|6|**Assault forces:** An assault ship from the Devastator has moved ahead and deployed a brigade of stormtroopers to secure the area.|

|1d6|North World XXX|
|---|---|
|1|**Bacchanal:** A couple who were alone in the steam rooms didn’t realize an evacuation had taken place, Upon coming out, they found the park empty. They are wandering around half-naked and disoriented.|
|2|**Demuestre su valor y gane una magnífica electro-hacha:** Si te pones delante de un tablero de madera y dejar que un maniquí lance chisporroteantes electro-hachas cerca de tus extremidades. Si no te mueves el maniquí te regala una de las hachas. Lo que no recordáis es de si está programado para el tamaño de un goblin.|
|3|**El espectáculo del dios del trueno:** Una maniquí disfrazado de algún tipo de dios mitológico sube al edificio más alto y empieza a lanzar desde sus manos arcos voltaicos que caen en diferentes partes seguras de la zona. Si alguien o algo estuviera en esas partes seguras, la descarga que recibiría sería mortal.|
|4|**Reconnaissance flight:** A spy shuttle from the Devastator flies over the area to report back to high command. If you are detected, your sabotage plan could fall apart.|
|5|**Silence:** By pure chance, at this moment there is no one nearby, neither people nor mannequins. You are alone and in complete silence.|
|6|**Los laureles de la victoria:** Ante la falta de visitantes, los maniquíes eléctricos empiezan a aglomerarse y a montar un desfile en honor a tus jugadores por sus logros en la batalla. Es difícil moverse entre la multitud que les vitorea y les lanza pétalos y guirnaldas.|

|---|---|
|1|**Acrobatic rider:** An electric horse trots along the main street, with a mannequin rider on its back performing highly dangerous stunts.|
|2|**Tumbleweeds:** Lots of desert tumbleweeds pass away.|
|3|**Duelo al sol:** Pasáis al lado del panel que controla el duelo de pistolas de rayos del atardecer. Si hay fuerzas de asalto del Devastador cerca, podríais tenderles una trampa con los maniquíes pistoleros o incluso lanzarlos programando a los maniquíes para que busquen y maten a los «forajidos» que vienen del Devastador. O simplemente pillar sus pistolas eléctricas por lo que pudiera pasar.|
|4|**Tónico de aceite de serpiente:** Un maniquí con levita negra y chistera se os acerca y os ofrece una botellita de cristal que dice que es tónico de aceite de serpiente. No para de repetir sus milagrosas virtudes. Lo extraño es que nadie recuerda haber ideado o programado estas funcionalidades nadie sabe que tiene la botellita en su interior.|
|5|**Electric horses:** At a watering trough, there are 4 electric horses with their charge at maximum capacity. Visitors use them to get around the area, and they are also perfect for pulling heavy loads.|
|6|**Three X:** The variety hall has just received a shipment of alcoholic beverages, including several large barrels containing high-proof spirits. These can be set on fire or used to craft effective Molotov cocktails.|

Os encontráis una pareja de esos pillastres saqueando los puestos de comida de la zona. Algo tendréis que hacer con ellos porque, si se quedan aquí, no saldrán bien parados.|

|5|**Rickshaws:** There’s a parking of rickshaws pulled by electric mannequins. You just have to hop on and tell them where you want to go, and the mannequin will take off at full speed.|
|6|**Helio:** Un maniquí eléctrico con una gran cantidad de globos de helio deambula cerca. Normalmente, se los da gratis a los viandantes y enseguida tienen que volver a inflar, pero ahora no hay nadie que lo quiera y empieza tener problemas para mantenerse sujeto al suelo.|

|1d6|South World|
|---|---|
|1|**Four wheeled:** In the middle of the forest, there is an off-road vehicle, likely left behind by a maintenance team, that can be used to move around the area more quickly.|
|2|**Quicksand:** A broken pipe has flooded an area of ​​sandstone, creating a type of quicksand that is not detectable at single sight.|

|6|**Stampede:** Something has triggered the area’s security system, causing all the animals to flee in a blind panic, moving away from the detected danger in order to save themselves.|

|1d6|Spaceport|
|---|---|
|1|**Cut off communication:** The Devastator is interfering with communications, and the control tower is struggling mightily to do its job.|
|2|**Lost luggage:** In their haste to escape, people are leaving their luggage behind wherever they happen to be. There are a couple of suitcases that promise interesting things inside.|
|3|**Seized engines:** A small service craft is unable to take off. If it does not receive assistance and repairs to its engines, its 3 crew members could be injured or even killed during the landing of the Devastator’s troops.|
